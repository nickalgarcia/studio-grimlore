The Oath at the Gilded Hall

Lady Verrin wants a public oath. The Iron Chapter wants an answer. The party wants to leave with their weapons.

▬ PREVIOUSLYSESSION 14 · 3 DAYS AGO

The Reliquary

The party went down into the Reliquary beneath the old capital carrying Halvard Stane's map, and found that someone had walked it first. Brix spotted fresh tallow on the stairs; Marrow's patron fell silent for the first time since Session 3.

In the lowest vault they recovered the Ashen Writ — intact but for its second seal, cut away cleanly within the week. Thessaly carries it now.

They surfaced to a summons. Lady Verrin requests the party at the Gilded Hall, unarmed, tomorrow night. Preceptor Aldwyn's riders were seen on the north road that same hour.
